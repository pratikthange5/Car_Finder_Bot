# 🚗 Dream Car Finder — AI-Powered Car Recommendation Chatbot

## 📌 Project Overview

**Dream Car Finder** is an AI-powered, no-code chatbot built using Botpress to help users find suitable cars based on their preferences, compare different models, estimate loan payments, and explore car images.

The chatbot uses uploaded car-company CSV datasets as a knowledge source to provide relevant vehicle information and assist users in making informed car-buying decisions.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Simplify the process of finding a suitable car.
- Help users discover vehicles based on their budget and preferences.
- Compare car specifications to support purchase decisions.
- Calculate estimated monthly loan payments and total interest.
- Provide relevant car images and useful reference links.
- Offer personalized recommendations based on user requirements.

## ✨ Key Features

### 1. 🔍 Find Your Ideal Car

Users can explore suitable cars based on:

- Budget and price range
- Brand and manufacturer
- Seating capacity
- Fuel type
- Body style
- Vehicle features
- Car segment

The chatbot provides available vehicle information, specifications, prices, features, safety ratings, and CarDekho links where supported by the available data and configured sources.

### 2. ⚖️ Compare Cars

- Compare two car models side by side.
- Review their available specifications, features, and prices.
- Identify the advantages of each model.
- Receive a recommendation based on the user's preferences and comparison criteria.

### 3. 💰 EMI Calculator

The chatbot helps users estimate car loan payments using relevant financial inputs.

- Monthly EMI estimation
- Total interest payable
- Total loan repayment
- Basic car financing guidance

The estimates depend on the entered loan amount, interest rate, and repayment tenure.

### 4. 🖼️ Car Images

- Help users explore vehicle images.
- Provide Google Images links to view relevant car photographs.

### 5. 🤖 Smart Car Recommendations

The chatbot suggests suitable vehicles based on requirements such as:

- Family use
- Fuel efficiency
- Luxury and comfort
- Budget constraints
- Seating requirements
- Other specific preferences

### 6. 🌐 Vehicle Information and References

The chatbot uses its configured knowledge sources and workflows to answer vehicle-related questions. Where live sources are configured, it can retrieve current information; otherwise, answers depend on the available dataset and knowledge sources.

## 🛠️ Technology Stack

- **Botpress:** No-code chatbot development and visual workflow design
- **CSV datasets:** Source data for vehicle information
- **Knowledge Base:** Retrieval of relevant information from uploaded sources
- **Conversational AI:** Natural-language interaction and recommendations
- **Workflow Logic:** User input handling and feature navigation

## 🧪 Testing and Validation

The chatbot was tested against six scenarios:

1. Finding cars under a specified budget.
2. Filtering cars by fuel type and seating capacity.
3. Comparing two car models using their specifications.
4. Handling requests for cars absent from the dataset.
5. Handling missing vehicle prices honestly.
6. Verifying EMI calculations against the applicable interest rate and loan tenure.

All six scenarios passed the initial tests performed for the project.

## 🚀 Example User Queries

Users can interact with Dream Car Finder using natural-language questions such as:

- "Show cars under ₹10 lakh."
- "Find the best 7-seater SUV."
- "Compare Hyundai Creta vs Kia Seltos."
- "Calculate EMI for Tata Nexon."
- "Show me BMW X5 photos."
- "Suggest an electric car under ₹20 lakh."
- "Find a fuel-efficient car for my family."

Recommendations and responses depend on the information available in the configured data sources and workflows.
